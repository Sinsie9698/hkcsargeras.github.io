Store Policy

Where our merch comes from

Hello Kitty Club merchandise is sold through our partner store at arma.gg. The "Store" link on this site takes you there.

Orders, payments and shipping

Arma handles every part of an order: checkout, payment, printing, shipping and delivery. We never see or store your payment details, and your purchase is made with Arma under their terms of sale.

Returns, refunds and problems with an order

Returns, exchanges, refunds, sizing, damaged items and missing deliveries are covered by Arma's own policies. Please contact Arma's customer support directly for anything to do with an order.

Questions about the designs

If you have ideas for new merch or questions about our designs, let an officer know on our Discord server.

Effective October 22, 2026.

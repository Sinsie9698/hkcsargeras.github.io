GUILD MEMBERS

Hello Kitty Club has been home to some of the best players in World of Warcraft. Over the years many have come and gone or moved on from the game altogether, but there are still many that call HKC home. Our community is built on a foundation of respect, teamwork, and a shared passion for raiding excellence.

HKC Leadership

Hello Kitty Club is comprised of the Guild Leader, Raid Leader, and Officers. The Guild Leader works hand in hand with the guild in all aspects of day to day operations, assists with recruiting and social media outreach, and after officer core advisement acts as the final say in guild decisions. The Raid Leader serves as second in command for guild decisions as well as the organization and leadership of the raid team. The Officers serve the guild in all aspects varying from raid leadership to monitoring the guild bank.

Our leadership team has many years of Warcraft logged in-game and are true masters of their respective classes and roles. If you ever have a question about the guild, these guys have the answer.

HKC Membership

Hello Kitty Club has deep membership built over 18 years and as such there are many that call HKC home in World of Warcraft.

Raider Kitty

Our active raiding roster comprised of dedicated players who participate in Heroic and Mythic activities. Members at this rank have guild bank access via officer approval as well as guild bank repairs.

Trial Kitty

New applicants are assigned our Trial Kitty rank. Depending on performance and fit with the guild culture, you will be added to our raiding roster or casual community.

Casual Kitty

Retired raiders, casual players, and friends/family members that want to be a part of our community. Being a member of our community means you are always welcome in Hello Kitty Club.

Our Community

What makes Hello Kitty Club special is not just our raiding achievements, but the relationships we've built over nearly two decades. We pride ourselves on maintaining a friendly, supportive, and inclusive environment where players can grow both mechanically and personally.

Our members come from all walks of life, and our guild has been a place where people have graduated high school, finished college, gotten married, had children, and watched their kids join the guild. We've celebrated real-world achievements together, supported each other through difficult times, and created memories that last far beyond the game.

If you're looking for a guild where you'll be treated with respect, encouraged to improve, and genuinely welcomed as a member of our family, Hello Kitty Club might be perfect for you.

GUILD MEMBERS

Hello Kitty Club has been home to some of the best players in World of Warcraft. Over the years many have come and gone or moved on from the game altogether, but there are still many that call HKC home. Our community is built on a foundation of respect, teamwork, and a shared passion for raiding excellence.

JOIN US TODAY

HKC Leadership

Hello Kitty Club is comprised of the Guild Leader, Raid Leader, and Officers. The Guild Leader works hand in hand with the guild in all aspects of day to day operations. The Raid Leader serves as second in command for guild decisions as well as the organization and leadership of the raid team. The Officers serve the guild in all aspects varying from raid leadership to monitoring the guild bank.

Our leadership team has many years of Warcraft logged in-game and are true masters of their respective classes and roles.

HKC Membership

Hello Kitty Club has deep membership built over 18 years. There are many ranks within our community:

Raider Kitty

Our active raiding roster of dedicated players who participate in Heroic and Mythic activities. Guild bank access available.

Trial Kitty

New applicants start here. Depending on performance and fit with guild culture, you'll be promoted or added to casual community.

Casual Kitty

Retired raiders, casual players, and friends/family. Always welcome in Hello Kitty Club regardless of activity level.

Our Community

What makes Hello Kitty Club special is the relationships we've built over nearly two decades. We maintain a friendly, supportive, and inclusive environment where players grow both mechanically and personally.

Our members have graduated high school, finished college, gotten married, had children, and watched their kids join the guild. We've celebrated real-world achievements together, supported each other through difficult times, and created memories that last far beyond the game.

If you're looking for a guild where you'll be treated with respect, encouraged to improve, and genuinely welcomed as family, Hello Kitty Club might be perfect for you.

Privacy Policy

The short version

This website does not have accounts, does not run analytics or advertising, and does not collect or store personal information itself.

Information you give us

When you apply to the guild, send a report or leave feedback, you do so through Google Forms. The answers you choose to give, such as your character name, BattleTag or Discord name, are stored by Google and seen by guild leadership only for the purpose of the form: reviewing your application, handling a report or improving the guild. Reports and feedback can be submitted anonymously. We do not sell or share this information.

Information collected by other services

Some parts of the site load content from other companies, and those companies may collect information such as your IP address and browser details under their own privacy policies:

  • Website hosting: our hosting provider may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses and pages visited, for security and reliability.
  • Twitch and YouTube: the video players on the Gallery page are provided by Twitch and YouTube. They only load when you open the Videos tab, and YouTube videos use YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode.
  • Google Forms: used for applications, reports and feedback.
  • Linked sites: Discord, social media, ranking sites and our merchandise store only receive information if you click through to them.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Your choices

If you would like an application or other form response you sent us to be deleted, ask an officer on our Discord server and we will remove it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date.

Effective October 22, 2026.

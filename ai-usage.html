AI Usage

How AI was used

Parts of this website were built with the help of AI tools, including the page layouts and code, converting our Guidelines, Policies & Procedures from a PDF into a web page, and drafting these policy pages. Everything was reviewed by guild leadership before being published.

What was not written by AI

The guild history, Blizzcon stories, guild guidelines and policies, screenshots and artwork were written, taken or commissioned by Hello Kitty Club members and artists. Where they were moved over from our old website or documents, they were copied word for word.

Statistics

Raid progression and ranking numbers are gathered automatically from Warcraft Logs and Raider.IO, not generated by AI, but they may still contain errors or be out of date.

Questions or corrections

If you spot a mistake anywhere on the site, please let us know through our feedback form or an officer on our Discord server.

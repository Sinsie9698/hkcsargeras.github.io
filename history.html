Guild History

A Legacy of Excellence Since 2006

2006

Guild Founded

Hello Kitty Club was established as a haven for players seeking a friendly, dedicated raiding community. From the beginning, HKC has been committed to pushing challenging content while maintaining a welcoming atmosphere.

Through the Years

Consistent Progression

Across multiple expansions and raid tiers, HKC has maintained a strong reputation for mythic progression and guild longevity. Our members have celebrated numerous cutting-edge achievements and conquered some of WoW's most challenging content.

Blizzcon

HKC at Blizzcon

Guild members have represented HKC at Blizzcon, strengthening our community connections and celebrating our achievements with the broader WoW community. These experiences have enriched our guild culture and brought us closer together.

Today

Present Day

HKC continues to thrive as an active, progressing mythic guild. We maintain our values of excellence, community, and fun. Our members form the heart of the guild, and we're always looking for talented players who want to be part of our legacy.

Notable Achievements

Mythic Progression

Multiple tiers of mythic raid progression and cutting-edge achievements across multiple expansions.

Community Focus

Known for creating a welcoming environment where players of all backgrounds feel valued and included.

Guild Longevity

Nearly two decades of continuous operation, demonstrating stability and commitment to our values.

Leadership Excellence

Dedicated officers and leadership team committed to fostering a positive, organized guild environment.

Our Values

Excellence

We strive for progression and improvement in everything we do, from raid performance to community building.

Community

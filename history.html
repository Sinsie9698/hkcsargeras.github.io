GUILD HISTORY

Founded on December 14, 2006, Hello Kitty Club began as an alt and PvP guild inspired by Melona for the raiding guild Unleashed. By summer 2007, HKC transitioned into a full raiding guild and quickly became one of the top-performing guilds on Akama throughout The Burning Crusade, Wrath of the Lich King, Mists of Pandaria, Warlords of Draenor, and Legion.

In 2017, HKC transferred to Sargeras, where we continued our tradition of excellence—clearing Mythic content in Legion, Battle for Azeroth, Shadowlands, Dragonflight and in The War Within, with a focused, semi-hardcore environment.

The Burning Crusade

The Melona/Aurongodx Years

During the summer of 2007, a guild named "Shattered" from the realm Azshara was struggling to make progress through tier 5 content. The guild led by Deltwarrior, Daelin, Kanlar, Aurongodx, and others decided to transfer off Azshara with the hopes of better raid progression on a higher population realm.

With Recently Bandaged now disbanded, the Azshara transplants now had no place to call home. Meanwhile, Melona and Soulsphere saw this as an opportunity. Melona had a guild called "Hello Kitty Club" that she created during the days of Vanilla World of Warcraft. She recruited the Azshara members and made Aurongodx, Soulsphere, and Meowmimi officers. Hello Kitty Club as a raiding guild was born.

Hello Kitty Club had immediate success clearing farm content and quickly became one of the few guilds on Akama to down Lady Vashj and Kael'thas Sunstrider. With its first Illidan kill, HKC was also the first Alliance guild on Akama to get a Warglaive. Shortly after defeating Illidan for the first time, Melona decided to step down as Guild Master and she passed the power to Aurongodx.

With the release of the Sunwell on April 8th, 2008, Hello Kitty Club wanted to cement its place at the top of Akama raid progression charts. During the guilds first 2 nights of attempts against Kalecgos, HKC achieved a server second kill. Brutallus proved to be a large step up in difficulty. Hello Kitty Club had the advantage of having two Warglaive rogues and achieved server second kill. Felmyst proved to be the boss that would stop guild progression.

After content nerfs, Hello Kitty Club quickly cleared through the remaining Sunwell bosses. HKC managed to defeat Kil'jaeden prior to the release of Wrath of the Lich King and were one of only two guilds on Akama to do so during the expansion.

Wrath of the Lich King

The Aurongodx/Warant Years

Wrath of the Lich King released on November 13th, 2008. With the launch of the expansion, the raiding landscape changed with 10man and 25man versions of raids. Hello Kitty Club had a strong roster leading into Wrath. Within the first week of launch, HKC had defeated Sartharion and many bosses in Naxx on 10man. By the second week, the guild had enough members to begin 25man raiding.

Looking to capitalize on the success, Hello Kitty Club was poised to push for top server kills in the next tier of content. Ulduar was released April 15th, 2009 as a massive 14 boss raid. The Ulduar raid team was led by Aurongodx with the help of Beastcloud. Throughout the tier, the guild managed to full clear both the 10man and 25man version of Ulduar along with many of the hard mode achievements.

Trial of the Crusader and Icecrown Citadel followed. Hello Kitty Club managed to full clear 10man & 25man normal, as well as 10man heroic Icecrown Citadel. By the time Hello Kitty Club defeated the Lich King on 10man normal difficulty, Aurongodx made the decision to step down from guild master. Leadership was passed on to Warant and the guild continued its strong performance.

Cataclysm & The Dark Years

The Warant/Beastcloud Years

Cataclysm released December 9th, 2010. Everything in Cataclysm was hard. The 5man heroics, the raids, and group content all proved to be a challenge. Hello Kitty Club managed to progress well at the start of the tier, full clearing all 3 raids on normal difficulty.

Server imbalance issues and recruitment difficulties led Warant to decide it was a lost cause. However, those in guild that still wanted to play the game convinced Warant to transfer leadership to Beastcloud. With new leadership and a new raid, Beastcloud prepared the guild for "Firelands". Unfortunately, Hello Kitty Club had a very poor showing and Beastcloud decided to shut the guild down and go inactive.

Throughout the remaining Firelands content and Dragonsoul, Hello Kitty Club did not raid. An unknown member eventually took control of the guild and invited their friends, using it as a bank alt. Thus, Hello Kitty Club was lost to the void…or was it?

Mists of Pandaria - The HKC Revival

Aurongodx Returns & The HKC Resurgence

Mists of Pandaria launched September 25th, 2012. Aurongodx had returned to World of Warcraft after taking a hiatus. When his guild "Learning Curve" fell apart, he reached out to old friends and made a play to revive Hello Kitty Club. After reclaiming the guild for just 20k gold from a player trying to sell it, Aurongodx was the Guild Master of Hello Kitty Club once again.

Hello Kitty Club managed to clear all of the 5.0 raid content on normal mode difficulty. During this time, HKC pursued Challenge Modes and completed all 9 with gold medals. HKC was the second alliance guild to accomplish this feat.

Patch 5.4 brought Flex Mode raids and Siege of Orgrimmar. Hello Kitty Club achieved a few server first boss kills on flex and ended in 5th place server wide. Through recruitment and strategic planning, HKC became the first alliance guild on the connected Akama realms to record a 25man kill of Garrosh Hellscream.

Warlords of Draenor to Present

Warlords of Draenor launched November 13th, 2014. Hello Kitty Club continued its tradition of clearing Mythic content while maintaining a friendly, semi-hardcore environment throughout the expansion.

In 2017, Hello Kitty Club made the decision to transfer to Sargeras to escape the declining population on Akama. After over 10 years on Akama, the guild carried its legacy and rich history to a thriving, high-population server. This marked a new chapter for HKC.

Since the transfer to Sargeras, Hello Kitty Club has continued to achieve Ahead of the Curve every raid tier and has earned Cutting Edge achievements in multiple expansions. We are currently raiding Mythic content on Sargeras and continue to welcome new members.

Our 18-year journey is a testament to the dedication of our members. Hello Kitty Club is not just a guild—it's a family that has grown together through countless raids and unforgettable moments in World of Warcraft.

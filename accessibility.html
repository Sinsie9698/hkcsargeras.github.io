Accessibility

Our commitment

We want everyone in our community to be able to use this website, including people who use screen readers, keyboards or other assistive technology.

Accessibility features

  • Readable layout: light text on dark backgrounds with gold headings, a clear page structure, and pages that resize for phones and tablets.
  • Keyboard friendly: every link and button can be reached with the Tab key, and the photo viewer closes with the Esc key.
  • Text descriptions: photos, boss kill screenshots, character portraits and logos include text descriptions for screen readers.
  • Reduced motion: if your device is set to reduce motion, animations and the home page slideshow movement are toned down.
  • Zoom: pages can be zoomed in your browser without losing content.
  • Plain-language pages: our guild policies are published as a normal web page rather than only as a PDF.

Known limitations

  • Some images contain text, such as the Guild Masters table and Guild Application graphic.
  • The embedded Twitch and YouTube video players and linked third-party sites follow their own accessibility standards.

Feedback

If something on the site is hard to use, please tell us through our feedback form or an officer on our Discord server, and we will do our best to fix it.

Effective October 22, 2026.
